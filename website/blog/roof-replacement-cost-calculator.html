How Our Roof Cost Calculator Works

Most online roof calculators ask for your home's square footage and multiply it by an average price. That skips the things that actually move a roofing price: how big the roof really is, how steep it is, and how many valleys and hips it has. Our calculator starts with your real roof.

We measure your roof

Satellite imagery maps every roof plane on your home, along with its area and pitch.

You tell us about the job

Pick a new roof or roof rejuvenation, then answer a few quick questions about stories and timing.

You see your price

We apply our actual pricing and show a range for every option, from rejuvenation to standing seam metal.

Because it measures each plane separately, the calculator catches details a square-footage guess misses: a steep front gable, a low-slope porch that needs a different material, or a complex roof that takes more labor and creates more cut waste.

How to Estimate Your Roof Size Yourself

Want to check our math, or compare a quote you already have? Here's how roofers turn a house into a number of squares.

1. Start with the roof's footprint

Measure the area the roof covers from above, including the garage, porches and the overhang past the walls. Your home's living space is not the same number: a 2,000 sq ft two-story house might only have a 1,100 sq ft footprint.

2. Multiply by the pitch factor

A sloped roof has more surface than the flat footprint under it. Multiply the footprint by the factor for your roof's pitch (rise in inches per 12 inches of run):

PitchMultiplierPitchMultiplier
3/121.0317/121.158
4/121.0548/121.202
5/121.0839/121.250
6/121.11810/121.302

3. Convert to squares and add waste

Divide the roof area by 100 to get squares, then add 10–15% for waste from cuts at valleys, hips and edges. More complex roofs need more.

Roof footprint, including garage and overhangs2,000 sq ft
× 6/12 pitch factor (1.118)2,236 sq ft
÷ 100 = roof squares22.4 squares
+ 12% waste = material to orderabout 25 squares

Most roofs have more than one pitch, which is why hand calculations drift. Our calculator measures every plane individually, so you don't have to.

Replace or Rejuvenate? Estimate Both

Not every aging roof needs to come off. If your asphalt shingles are dull and drying out but still intact, roof rejuvenation restores flexibility to the shingles you already have and can add years of life for a fraction of what a replacement costs. Pick Roof Rejuvenation in the calculator to see that price next to your replacement options.

Roof rejuvenationRoof replacement
What happensClean, make small repairs, apply a treatment to your existing shinglesTear off the old roof and install a new system
Best forAging but sound asphalt roofs with no leaks or hail damageRoofs with cracking, curling, leaks, hail fractures or soft decking
CostA fraction of replacementFull replacement price
What you getTypically 5–10 more years; product makers recommend reapplying about every 5 yearsA new roof with new manufacturer warranties
⚠️
Rejuvenation isn't right for every roof. It can't fix leaks, hail fractures, missing shingles or a rotted deck, and a treatment can limit parts of an active shingle warranty. We inspect first and check your warranty before recommending it. If your roof needs replacing, we'll tell you.

Why the Calculator Gives a Range

Satellite measurements are very good at size and pitch. What they can't see is what's under your shingles. A few things can only be confirmed when we're on the roof:

  • Decking: soft or rotted plywood is replaced sheet by sheet as needed.
  • Hidden layers: some homes have an extra layer of old shingles that isn't obvious from the ground.
  • Ventilation: poor attic ventilation shortens a roof's life and may need to be corrected.
  • Flashing: worn chimney, wall and pipe flashing is replaced so the new roof stays watertight.

When the satellite imagery for your street is lower resolution, the range widens a little to stay honest. Your free inspection locks in the final number. There's no pressure and no cost.

Ways to Lower Your Roof Replacement Cost

  • Compare materials side by side. The calculator prices every option for your exact roof, so you can see what an upgrade really costs.
  • Ask your insurer about impact-resistant shingles. Some insurers offer a premium discount for Class 4 roofs. Check with your agent before you decide.
  • Use financing. Monthly payment options let you replace a failing roof now instead of paying for leak damage later. Check the box in the calculator to see an example payment.
  • Don't replace a roof that still has life left. If your shingles are aging but intact, roof rejuvenation may buy you years at a fraction of the cost. The calculator prices it for your roof.
  • Compare quotes the right way. Make sure every quote covers the same tear-off, underlayment, ventilation and warranty. Our guide to choosing a roofing contractor walks through what to ask.
